# Production release 2026-10-05-30

Recorded 2026-10-16 (UTC+8). Worker e2b92c3f-09e4-4ca6-8e54-3fe20d249159.

The founder asked for this in chat: play the game as different kinds of players over several rounds, decide the improvements, build them and release them to production.

## How it was played

All play happened on a local chain with the exact v3 contracts and real drand rounds. It used ganache development wallets and one fresh local wallet; no real or testnet funds were involved. Two scripted "other players" summoned, dived, collected and listed cards in the background, so the live log, the market and the summon feed had real activity.

Four kinds of players:
- **New player:** from no wallet and no gas to the first collected result.
- **Optimizer:** three teams, dispatch all, every decision type, collect all, claim, the daily limits.
- **Trader:** buying from other players, approvals, batch listing.
- **Returning player:** coming back to a revealed result.

## What was wrong, and what changed

Problems found in the live game (release 29):
- **Unstyled panels.** The transaction panel, the unsaved-teams bar and the summons-to-open bar were plain text in a corner, overlapping the side rail.
  - Fix: they now have proper panels. Transactions show bottom right with a status per step; the bars sit centred at the bottom.
- **Wrong network on connect.** Connecting a wallet on `/game/lightdive` asked the wallet to switch to Robinhood Chain mainnet. The site's wallet dialog then said "wallet is on another network" and offered a mainnet switch that broke the game.
  - Fix: on the live game page, connecting and the wallet dialog now use the game's chain (the testnet until the mainnet switch). Every other page is unchanged.
- **Preview text and simulated data.** Several places still said "preview mode / simulated" in the live game: the sell dialog, batch listing, listing detail, live log, card supply, the treasury pool panel and the share card.
  - Fix: that text now shows only in the preview; the live game says what actually happens.
  - The summon page's "Summons across the server" feed was simulated data. It now reads the latest Minted events from the chain, and your own summons show as "you".
- **Price wording.** The summon page said prices update daily from the pool average. On chain the price is set by the contract (it can only move within its bounds, at most 10% a day, and there is no price mover yet), so the wording now says that and shows the current prices.
- **Text fixes:**
  - English and Korean names ran together ("VeinSpire"); there is now a space.
  - "1 Berths" is now "1 berth(s)".
  - The live log's top multiplier showed ×1.00 before any result; it now shows "—".
  - Rovyn kept a reply line in the old language after a language switch; that line is now dropped.

New-player and flow improvements:
- **The observatory says what to do next, in order:**
  1. connect a wallet;
  2. switch network;
  3. get test ETH for gas;
  4. claim test $RVYN, in one click from the observatory;
  5. collect waiting results, in one click (previously it suggested a new dive for a team that could not dive until it collected);
  6. teams on the bridge, with the countdown;
  7. today's suggested route.

  The wallet strip is hidden on the observatory, since the main panel says the same thing.
- **After a summon:** a "next step" button appears — summon seekers, summon a spire, or build a team on the Stele Plain.
- **Teams:**
  - Teams with unsaved changes are marked "unsaved" instead of "ready".
  - Diving, or dispatching several teams, saves any unsaved team changes in the same flow, in the right order: teams taken apart first.
  - The bar's wording says so.
- **Test tokens:**
  - The wallet strip's button claims test $RVYN directly instead of only opening the page.
  - The test-token page puts the claim first for a wallet that already has gas.

Not changed: economy numbers, contracts and the founder's earlier decisions. There is still no one-click team building, which the founder rejected.

**No D1 migration and no onchain action.** No contract was changed or deployed. Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
