# Production release 2026-10-05-31

Recorded 2026-10-16 (UTC+8). Worker 328315b1-d9f8-4f1f-8f0a-943527f44a76. The founder delivered the 30 final seeker paintings and asked in chat to put them in the game and release ("好 先上").

## What changed

- **Seeker cards use the founder's finished art** everywhere a seeker is shown: collection, summon results, market, Stele Plain, voyage crew and NFT detail.
  - There are 30 paintings: 6 lights × 5 rarities, transparent PNG, 1024×1366.
  - `components/lightdive/game/art.tsx` (`SeekerArt`) places the painting on a glow in the seeker's light colour, so the light still reads at the higher rarities, and fades the bottom of the knee-up paintings into the card.
  - Spires and prisms keep their drawn art until their paintings arrive.
- **Game files:** `public/lightdive/cards/seeker-l{light}-r{rarity}.webp`, 30 files of about 1.3 MB in total, 600 px tall. They are made by the new `scripts/lightdive-art-webp.mjs` from the originals.
- **Originals and provenance:** the originals are archived in `docs/lightdive/art/incoming/card/`, replacing the 10-12 r0 trials. The tool, prompts, delivery record with SHA-256 per file and the cleanup record are in `docs/lightdive/art/incoming/prompts/seekers-final-2026-10-16*`, and one row per file was added to `docs/lightdive/art/incoming/LOG.md`.
- **Founder's choices:**
  - The Abysslight colour in the UI stays as it is for now, although the paintings lean indigo.
  - Glimmer seekers are machines on purpose.
  - Light-coloured backgrounds will come from the founder later.
- **Public repository:** the art is not published there. Both `docs/lightdive/art/incoming/` and `public/lightdive/cards/` stay off the MIT-licensed repository, so the code there falls back to missing images.

**No D1 migration and no onchain action.** Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
