# Production release 2026-10-05-33

Recorded 2026-10-16 (UTC+8). Worker e2e5ad89-7889-4ed9-86a1-f0f6f1b0fb27. This release is items A (footprints) and B (records and boards) of the gameplay-depth proposal (`docs/lightdive/GAMEPLAY-DEPTH-v1.md`). The founder chose them on 2026-10-16. Neither needs a contract change.

## What changed

- **New page 航海紀錄 (Logbook)** in the game's main menu (`components/lightdive/v3/logbook.tsx`).
  - **My records:** results collected, destinations visited (x/24), deepest reached, best multiplier, Legends and great-or-better results, and the daily streak.
  - **Titles:** 9 honorary titles from the records, for example the first result, reaching each cluster, a Legend, 7 days in a row, and all 24 destinations.
  - **Footprints:** two story fragments per destination, 48 in all.
    - The first opens with the first result collected there, the second with the fifth.
    - The text was written for the game's world and follows the story rules: literary, and no game-mechanic words.
    - It exists in four languages. The source is `docs/lightdive/FOOTPRINTS.json`; `scripts/lightdive-footprints.mjs` writes `lib/lightdive-v3/footprints.ts` and makes zh-Hans with the existing converter.
    - Newly opened fragments show as a badge on the menu item until the page is opened.
  - **This week's boards:** everyone's dives over the last 7 days, read from the chain (`readWeek` in `lib/lightdive-v3/onchain.ts`). Honours only: most voyages, deepest reached, best multiplier, Legends. There is deliberately no earnings board.
- **Preview:** the preview gets the same page on simulated data, and its weekly data waits for the clock to be set (it crashed on the first render).
- **Not on the chain:** nothing on this page pays anything. Titles, footprints and boards are computed in the browser from the player's own on-chain dives and the public dive events.
- **Translations:** 33 new interface strings in four languages.

**No D1 migration and no onchain action.** Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
