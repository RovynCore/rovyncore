## What changed on the site

- **Deep dive (深潛):**
  - Available on destinations from cluster ③ on: 2 Dive Passes, 15 minutes, Lightdust ×2, risk one level up.
  - The voyage decision comes early. In the last 10 minutes a "press on / turn back" card appears; turning back settles at ×1.00 with 1 wear.
  - A turned-back result is labelled 回航.
- **Crew bond (默契):**
  - The Stele Plain shows the bond as five blocks.
  - Destination cards and the voyage screen show it, and the odds include it.
- **Voyage supplies (出航補給):**
  - A dialog after 出發 and after 一次派遣 offers lamp oil (4%, 1 wear) or a star chart (6%, empty-handed −3 points). It shows the price and the change in empty-handed chance.
  - When needed, the first purchase adds one approval for the pool. Payment is 95% to the pool and 5% burned.
- **Daily commissions (每日委託):**
  - A new menu page with a badge, plus a card on the observatory.
  - Accept, complete and abandon are one button each. Completing also collects the qualifying result if it is still waiting, by revealing it in the same go.
- The guide has a new "更多玩法" section. The whitepaper (zh-Hant, en, ko, plus zh-Hans converted) explains the four mechanics and lists supplies in the fee table.
- **Preview:** the simulated preview has all four mechanics.
- **Translations:** v3.1 strings in four languages (`lib/lightdive-v3/i18n/v31.ts`).

**No D1 migration.** Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
