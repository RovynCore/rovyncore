# Production release 2026-10-05-35

Recorded 2026-10-17 (UTC+8). Worker 3b6462ec-9e9c-4755-91cb-7ac10044d733. Layout fixes from a full walk through the game, which the founder asked for on 2026-10-17. The change is UI only: no contract change.

## What changed

- **Voyage decision on screens up to 1180 px wide:**
  - At those widths the decision covers the whole screen. It used to render inside the voyage scene, which is its own stacking context, so the menu rail, the resource bar and the voyage log were drawn over it and hid its left half.
  - It now renders at the game root (`StageLayer` in `voyage.tsx`). Like the other overlays, it starts right of the menu rail. Wider screens are unchanged: there the decision covers only the scene.
- **Menu rail:** the rail always had a sideways scrollbar, caused by the volume pop-up even while hidden. The pop-up is now narrower, and the rail no longer scrolls sideways.
- **Voyage scene:** a sideways scrollbar came from the backdrop bleeding 4% past the edges. It is gone now.
- **Summon odds:** the rarity name column was a fixed 52 px, sized for two Chinese characters. English and Korean names (Prismatic, Corestar, 무지갯빛) ran into the next column. The rows now share one column sized to the longest name, using subgrid.
- **Tutorial:**
  - Step 3 points at "the destination card" instead of "on the right".
  - Step 4 is titled "出發之後：航行畫面", because it shows before the player departs.
  - The last step no longer shows "skip" next to "got it".
  - Translations were updated in four languages.

**No D1 migration and no onchain action.** Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
