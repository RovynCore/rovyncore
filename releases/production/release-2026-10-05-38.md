# Production release 2026-10-05-38

Recorded 2026-10-19 (UTC+8). Worker 6a9b428d-0de8-449c-b14b-0ee9fac53a1a. The founder asked on 2026-10-19 for three things: step-by-step unlocks; titles that others can see, with the personal records page grown into a profile; and an operating-data view in the admin. After building them, the founder asked for two verification passes playing as different kinds of user. This release ships the work and the fixes from those passes. There is no contract change.

## What changed

- **Step-by-step unlocks** (`components/lightdive/v3/unlocks.tsx`):
  - A new observer sees the core loop first.
  - The profile joins the menu after the first collected result.
  - Supplies and daily commissions open after the third. Before that, departing asks nothing, and the observatory shows no commission panel.
  - Deep dives open once a team can reach cluster ③.
  - A player who already used a feature keeps it.
  - Each unlock is announced once with Rovyn and a button to the feature. The first look only records what is already open, so existing players get no pile of notices.
  - Locked pages are only left out of the menu; routing still knows them, so a shared link opens.
- **Profile (個人檔案, grown out of 航海紀錄):**
  - The page shows the featured title (the hardest title earned), the observer's best card as a full-art or foil card face, the career in 8 tiles, titles (earned first; the featured one highlighted), footprints and the weekly boards.
  - Anyone's profile opens at `#logbook=0x…`. In the live game, `chain.profile(address)` reads that wallet's public chain record; the chain-to-state mapping was extracted into `playerState()` in `use-chain.ts` for this.
  - Names in the weekly boards, the live dive log, today's ranking and the summon feed open that observer's profile.
  - The player's own board rows and the share image carry the featured title.
  - "Copy my profile link".
- **Admin operating numbers** (`lib/lightdive-v3/stats.ts`, `components/lightdive/v3/admin-stats.tsx`, on `/admin/lightdive`):
  - Everything is computed in the browser from the v3 contracts' public events. There is no database and no migration, and nothing private (wallet addresses only).
  - **Totals:** wallets, active today, next-day and day-7 retention, pool and rate, dives/deep/turn-backs, cards summoned and paid, rewards and departure costs, withdrawals and fees, burned, supplies, commissions, market sales and tax, faucet claims.
  - **A 14-day daily table** with CSV download.
  - **Shares as ten-block lines:** clusters, voyage decisions, outcomes and minted rarities.
  - The retired v2 contract tools are folded into a closed section, so they are not mistaken for v3. Announcements and the reward calculator stay on top.

## Fixes from the two verification passes

- **Round 1** covered eight kinds of user: a new player, a returning player, an English speaker, a collector, a trader, a phone user, the founder on the admin page, and a visitor arriving from a shared profile link. Fixes:
  - The menu group headings (主要 / 資產 …) stayed in English after the unlock filter; the menu now reads the language when it renders.
  - The welcome-back card lists $RVYN waiting in the Treasury.
  - Two profile tiles reused button wording in English. New labels: "Summons", "Commissions completed".
  - The card detail no longer repeats the rarity tag under the card face.
  - Rare cards (稜光, 曜核) get a slow holographic sweep in every card grid.
  - 渡 is larger on the market page.
  - A visitor without a wallet gets "回到觀測站" instead of "回到我的檔案", and the button no longer stretches.
  - Admin: CSV download, and the v2 tools are folded.
- **Round 2:**
  - A layout audit of all 17 views in zh-Hant, en and ko: no spill, no overflow, no untranslated text, no leftover "(s)".
  - The leaderboard check: the featured title shows beside "你".
  - The shared-profile check on a local chain.
  - No new issues.

**No D1 migration and no onchain action.** Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
