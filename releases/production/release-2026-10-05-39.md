Recorded 2026-10-19 (UTC+8). Worker 42ac43af-4f6f-45de-9d80-62966e513be8. The founder said to ship them straight away. Proposal B (echo bridges) needs a contract change; it is being built separately and will not be deployed until the founder decides. There is no contract change here.

## What changed

- **Card history (卡片履歷)** (`lib/lightdive-v3/history.ts`):
  - Each card's own record follows it from owner to owner: voyages, the deepest destination, best multiplier, great-or-better results, Legendaries, deep dives pressed to the end, the teams it sailed with, and its first voyage.
  - **Live game:** the record is read from public events alone. A team's roster at each dive is rebuilt from TeamSet and TeamDisbanded, and results come from Revealed (and DiveDetails, TurnedBack). It is read incrementally (only new blocks each refresh) and de-duplicated.
  - **Preview:** each dive now records its crew.
- **Marks, honour only:**
  - The six marks: 傳說見證 (a team it sailed with brought back a Legendary), 星冠見證 (reached ⑥), 深淵歸來 (a deep dive completed), 豐收常客 (10 great-or-better), 老兵 (30 voyages) and 續航者 (renewed).
  - They show as chips on collection and market cards, at most two.
  - The card detail and the market listing detail have a 卡片履歷 panel. It says that marks change no power, odds or reward.
- **Market listing detail:** it now shows the same card face (full-art foil for 稜光 and 曜核) as the card detail.
- **Light and beam emblems** (`public/lightdive/icons/`):
  - They appear beside every light and beam name: the trait line, collection filters, the Stele Plain filters and attunement list, seat labels, destination cards and labels, the voyage log header, the observatory route chip and the summon result.
- **Seeker card backgrounds** (`public/lightdive/cards/bg/`): full-art seekers (稜光, 曜核) stand in their own light's landscape; spires and prisms keep their kind's banner.
- **Records:** originals in `docs/lightdive/art/incoming/misc`, 15 rows in `LOG.md`, prompts and delivery records in `prompts/`. The art stays out of the public repository.

**No D1 migration and no onchain action.** Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
