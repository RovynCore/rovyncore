## Contract change (v3.2, unaudited)

- **Echo bridges (光橋回聲):**
  - Every dive leaves its bridge at the destination for the rest of the UTC day (`lastDiver`).
  - The next dive there by another observer is echoed: 1 point of empty-handed chance moves to "normal" at the reveal (`LightdiveConfig3.echoStability`, owner-settable from 0 to 5).
  - The observer who left the bridge is counted as its guide (`echoesGiven`, event `Echoed`). This is honour only; nothing is paid to anyone.
  - The commissions "good or better" check uses the same stability.
- **Size and ABI:** Expedition3 is 24,274 bytes (limit 24,576). The ABI only adds to v3.1's.
