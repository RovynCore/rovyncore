# Production release 2026-10-05-41

Recorded 2026-10-19 (UTC+8). Worker ae43a71c-3640-4bd8-81e4-f63cd8b247b2. This release adds proposal 1, 今日首航 (first arrival of the day), which the founder approved on 2026-10-19. It also fixes the star-map layout problems found in the founder-requested desktop and mobile pass. There is no contract change.

## What changed

- **今日首航 (first arrival of the day)** (`lib/lightdive-v3/history.ts` `firstsOf`):
  - Each destination shows who reached it first today: on the destination card, where the name links to the profile, and in the map label's details.
  - When nobody has been there yet, the card says so.
  - **Live game:** read from the public Dived events alone. It uses the incremental ledger already read for the card histories (the earliest event per day and destination).
  - A voyage that was the first of the day there carries a 今日首航 tag.
  - **Two profile titles, honour only:** 破曉者 (first arrival once) and 先行者 (30 times). Counts come from the chain for anyone's profile. The preview counts them from its simulated observers.
- **Star map layout (desktop):**
  - **Problem:** on 1024–1440px screens the cluster labels on the right (④ ⑤) ran past the map's edge under the live log, and on narrower maps the labels ran into each other.
  - **Galaxy labels** now sit centred under their orbs. The six clusters were repositioned, with ⑥ still at the centre.
  - **Maps narrower than 1000px:** labels are a size smaller.
  - **Maps narrower than 900px:**
    - The orbs are smaller.
    - Each label shows the name only; the power range and sky events move into the hover details.
  - **Hover details** no longer widen a label while hidden.
  - **Cluster view:** destination labels wrap at 200px on a narrow map, and ④D moved right.
  - **861–1180px screens:** the map takes the full width and the live log follows below it.
  - **Rovyn's docked line** on the star map shows only from 1600px wide, because below that it covered the map title.
  - Checked by script at 1024, 1366, 1440 and 1920: no label overlaps another label or orb, runs past the map, sits under the bottom bar or reaches into the title. This held on the galaxy and in all six clusters.
  - The mobile layout (≤860px) is unchanged; these rules are scoped to desktop.
- **Translations:** i18n `v35.ts` (7 rows).

**No D1 migration and no onchain action.** Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
