# Production release 2026-10-05-42

Recorded 2026-10-19 (UTC+8). Worker f8e2c0e6-8423-4587-be82-22c4aa9ee159. On 2026-10-19 the founder approved three things: proposal A, 觀測站日報 (the observatory daily) on the home page; proposal C, 觀測者名片 (the observer card); and the server-wide chat. There is no contract change.

## What changed

- **觀測站日報 on the home page** (`components/rv/daily-bulletin.tsx`, `lib/lightdive-v3/daily.ts`, `GET /api/lightdive/daily`):
  - Shows yesterday (UTC) in Lightdive: dives (and deep dives), observers out (and newcomers), Legendaries, the top multiplier with who and where, destinations reached of 24 (and the busiest), and echoes.
  - Rovyn adds a line from the numbers, and a button leads to the game.
  - **Data:** read from the public chain events alone. A day is stored in `settings` once its reveals are in (30 minutes after midnight), so each day is read from the chain only once.
  - It says the game is on the testnet with test tokens of no value. There are no reward figures and no promise of any return.
- **Home page game status:** it read "In development · First look coming to this site". It now reads "Testnet open · Lightdive, test tokens, no value" with a link to the game, because the game has been public on the testnet since 2026-10-16.
- **觀測者名片 (observer card)** (`components/lightdive/v3/profile-card.tsx`):
  - Your own profile can download a 1200×630 card, or post it to X with a link to the profile.
  - The card shows the featured title, six career numbers (including first arrivals) and the card that stands for the observer, painted on its own scene.
  - Share images now load with `onload` instead of `decode()`, which never settles in a hidden tab.
- **潛光頻道, the server-wide chat** (`components/lightdive/v3/chat.tsx`, `lib/lightdive-chat.ts`, `GET`/`POST /api/lightdive/chat`), **off in production until its tables exist:**
  - **One channel for every language.** Anyone can read it.
  - **Who can speak:** a wallet signed in with the site's existing sign-in (no gas) that holds at least one Lightdive card.
  - **Limits:** 200 characters, one message every 10 seconds, 200 a day.
  - **Refused:** links; any mention of private keys or recovery phrases (an anti-scam rule); a short list of slurs.
  - **Moderation:** in `/admin/lightdive`, the admin wallet can hide a message or mute a wallet. Each action is signed and written to the audit log.
  - **Cost control:** the latest 60 messages are kept at the edge for 3 seconds. The panel polls every 8 seconds while open and every 45 seconds while closed (for the unread count), and never while the tab is hidden.
  - Messages older than 30 days are deleted.
  - **New migration `drizzle/0003_lightdive_chat.sql`:** two new tables, `lightdive_chat` and `lightdive_chat_mutes`. Nothing existing is changed.
    - **It has NOT been applied to production.** The deploy tool refused it without the founder's explicit go-ahead, as the release rules require.
    - Until it is applied, `GET /api/lightdive/chat` answers `{ off: true }` (cached for a minute) and the chat button stays hidden.
    - Applying it turns the channel on with no redeploy.
- **Translations:** i18n `v36.ts` (card and daily) and `v37.ts` (chat, including the server's refusal messages).

**No D1 migration was applied and there was no onchain action.** Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
