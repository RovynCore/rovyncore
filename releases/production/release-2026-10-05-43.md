## What changed

- **Five channels:** global, 繁中, 简中, EN and 한국어. They are tabs in the panel, which remembers your choice. Each channel is cached at the edge on its own.
- **Anti-scam rules:** six rules (`CHAT_RULES` in `chat.tsx`). They open on a first visit and stay behind the 防詐規則 button. They cover:
  - the team never DMs you or asks for keys or signatures;
  - no links, and "claim or verify on a site" is a scam;
  - nobody can guarantee profit;
  - no private-messaging strangers;
  - trade cards only on the market;
  - report anything suspicious.
- **Filter** (`lib/lightdive-chat.ts`). It refuses:
  - links (including spaced-out domains);
  - contact handles and "DM me";
  - wallet secrets;
  - bait such as airdrops, guaranteed profit and managed trading;
  - people posing as staff;
  - a short list of slurs.

  It was checked against ordinary game talk in the four languages, for example "倍率翻倍了" and "委託完成，領到獎勵了": no false refusals. Ten typical scam lines were all refused.
- **Official mark:** messages from the admin wallet (`OWNER`) carry 官方 / Official. That wallet may post links and notices, and it skips the card rule and the rate limits.
- **Reports:**
  - A flag sits on every other observer's message, one report per wallet per message.
  - At 3 reports from different wallets the message is hidden until the admin looks.
  - The admin view shows each message's channel and its report count.
- **Translations:** 26 rows added to `i18n/v37.ts`, covering the rules and the server's refusal messages.

Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`). No onchain action.
