# Production release 2026-10-05-44

Recorded 2026-10-19 (UTC+8). Worker a75d845d-4350-453a-97d6-2c33bcf62874. On 2026-10-19 the founder asked for a UI pass from the player's point of view. This is its first round: every view was walked at 1366×768 (the common laptop size) and checked again at 375px. There is no contract change and no migration.

## What changed

- **每日委託 (daily commissions):**
  - Each kind of commission now has its own emblem and colour: a tinted icon tile, a top accent line and a tinted card.
  - The reward is a gold chip.
  - The action button is pinned to the bottom of the card. The three cards' buttons used to sit at different heights when one title wrapped.
- **個人檔案 (profile) titles:** a title that counts something (streak, places, bond, supply days, commissions, first arrivals, echoes) shows a progress bar with "now / goal" once you have started on it.
- **碑原 (Stele Plain) seats:** a light or beam name, such as 針芒 or 霧光, no longer breaks into a vertical column on a narrow seat. It drops under the power instead.
- **Line breaks:** paragraphs avoid leaving one or two characters alone on the last line (`text-wrap-style: pretty`), and headings balance. This applies in the game and on the site.
  - The first try used the `text-wrap` shorthand, which also overrides `nowrap`. That broke the fee-chart labels in the treasury and the brand line. It is now the longhand, which leaves `nowrap` alone, and both were checked again.
- **Page headers:** the title starts at the top even when the side holds a tall card. On the market, 渡's card used to push the title down.
- **Brand line:** "LIGHTDIVE · 預覽 v3" in the menu stays on one line.
- **Chat button:**
  - It is now a 48px round icon with the unread badge, so it covers less of a page, for example the last line of the voyage log.
  - The word 頻道 shows on hover.

Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`). No D1 migration, no onchain action.
