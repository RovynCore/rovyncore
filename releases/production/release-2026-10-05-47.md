## What changed

- **Decision card:** it now names its voyage beside "航行抉擇", for example 光塔 #3 · ④D 霧心. With several teams out after a batch dispatch, a player could not tell which voyage a decision belonged to.
- **Chat button:** it steps aside while any dialog is open (batch dispatch, a card, a purchase). It used to cover the right end of a dialog's main button.
- **Preview market:** a simulated seller's card has worn voyages but no history in the preview. The history panel now explains that instead of saying the card has never sailed. The live game reads real histories from the chain.
- **Translations:** i18n `v39.ts` (1 row).

Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`). No D1 migration, no onchain action.
