# Production release 2026-10-05-48

Recorded 2026-10-21 (UTC+8). Worker b557a62c-9667-4482-b35d-17f6d623db35.

This release answers the founder's 10-21 request, in one batch deployed only after all of it was done:
- a full interface check, so boxes stop huddling to one side;
- a smaller first load;
- a profile the player can choose what to show on;
- a postcard-quality share image;
- a designed voyage narrative with much more text.

There is no contract change, no migration and no onchain action.

## What changed

### Layout
- A fill layer for tile rows, stat grids and card lists: items share a row's width instead of leaving a gap on one side.
  - Column counts step down by width.
  - A lone last item in a two-column grid spans the row.
- Team rows show their crew as small portraits.
- A scan of 15 pages at 1366px found no rows left squeezed.

### First load
- The Lightdive dictionary is split into one file per language (`scripts/lightdive-i18n-split.mjs`, run on every build).
  - A Traditional Chinese player downloads none of it.
  - Other players download only their own language, through `<GameLocale>`.
- The whitepaper loads only when it is opened.

### 自訂檔案 (the profile chooser)
- On one's own profile, the player can choose:
  - the featured title, from the titles earned;
  - the featured card;
  - the scene behind the profile: the card's scene, the star map, the six clusters, two observatory views or six seeker landscapes;
  - up to 8 numbers, from 20, in the order picked;
  - a motto, from 12 preset lines;
  - which sections show (titles, footprints, weekly boards) and their order.
- The choices are kept per wallet in localStorage, on that device only.
  - Anyone else who opens the profile sees the defaults.
- The featured title also appears on the reveal's share image.

### 觀測者明信片 (the postcard)
- The share image is a postcard, 1800×1200 landscape or 1200×1800 portrait.
- **Picture side:**
  - the chosen scene;
  - the featured card's painting with a glow in its rarity colour;
  - "Greetings from" the deepest place reached;
  - a gold title plate.
- **Back:**
  - a perforated stamp of the deepest cluster, with a dated postmark;
  - the motto on ruled lines, with a line of the career;
  - six chosen numbers;
  - From / To lines.
- The player sees a preview before downloading.

### Voyage narrative
- A voyage log now has about 15 beats, up from 9:
  1. departure, then Rovyn's line
  2. the team's state: followed a bridge, first arrival, deep dive, lamp oil, star chart, bond, or none
  3. the place
  4. a seeker
  5. the readings
  6. the decision
  7. attunement, or the cluster
  8. the sky
  9. the place again
  10. a seeker
  11. the deep descent (deep dives that keep going)
  12. the approach by cluster, then the general approach
  13. the closing
  14. the omen
  15. the log
- **The readings** follow the voyage's real empty-handed chance after every modifier: steady at 6% or less, even at 18% or less, hard above that. The team's state and attunement lines also follow the voyage's real inputs.
- **The omen** is written only once the result exists:
  - empty, partial and normal results get one line;
  - good and great get two;
  - a legend gets three rising lines;
  - turning back gets its own line.
  - The reveal's signal scan shows the omen one line at a time.
- **The words never change the odds.**
- 265 new lines are in `docs/lightdive/TEXTS-v3-draft.md` section 八:
  - 72 lines across the 24 destinations;
  - seekers, sky events and choices: 8, 6 and 6 lines each;
  - 21 team-state lines;
  - 9 readings lines;
  - 12 attunement lines;
  - 18 deep-descent lines;
  - 18 approach lines;
  - 33 omen lines.
- `scripts/lightdive-v3-texts.mjs` parses the new sections.
- **Translations:**
  - `i18n/voyage2.ts` (265 rows) and `i18n/v40.ts` (61 rows).
  - Simplified Chinese was made with `scripts/lightdive-hant2hans.mjs`.
  - 0 strings are missing in any language.

Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
