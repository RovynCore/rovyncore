# Production release 2026-10-05-51

Recorded 2026-10-22 (UTC+8). Worker d307aa57-87ef-4c6e-9857-d5be9158b57b.

This release wires in the founder's P3 supplement art: 14 images, made with the built-in image_gen tool. With it, all 88 images in the 10-21 art list are in the game. There is no contract change, no migration and no onchain action.

## What changed

- **6 deep-dive layers** (`public/lightdive/deep/deep-1..6.webp`)
  - When a deep dive keeps going past its destination, the voyage backdrop switches to the layer below its cluster at the descent point. The descent point is `DESCENT_AT` = 0.47, the same moment the deep-descent lines appear in the log.
  - The WebGL scene is rebuilt once, with the new picture.
  - A dive that turns back keeps its destination painting.
- **2 Rovyn poses: cheer and comfort** (`public/lightdive/npc/rovyn-pose-cheer.webp` and `rovyn-pose-comfort.webp`)
  - `rovynFor` in pages.tsx picks Rovyn's reaction to a result:

    | Result | Pose |
    |---|---|
    | Great, legend | cheer |
    | Good | bright |
    | Normal, turned back | guide |
    | Empty, partial | comfort |

  - The reaction shows above the reveal dialog's title and on the result share image.
  - The observatory's Rovyn now cheers after a recent great or legend result, and comforts after a recent empty one.
- **6 postcard stamps** (`public/lightdive/stamp/stamp-1..6.webp`)
  - The postcard's stamp now shows the deepest cluster's own stamp art. It used to be a crop of a background.

Originals are in `docs/lightdive/art/incoming/misc/{deep,stamp}` and `npc/`. LOG.md has 14 rows. `scripts/lightdive-art-webp.mjs` gained two jobs; the Rovyn poses go through the existing npc job.

Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
