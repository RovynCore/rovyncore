# Production release 2026-10-05-52

Recorded 2026-10-22 (UTC+8). Worker 08446b4f-81be-439e-998d-30ad8314b880.

This release makes three changes the founder asked for on 10-22. There is no contract change, no migration and no onchain action.

## What changed

### Step-by-step unlocks
- **The daily commissions are open from the start.** `unlocksOf` in unlocks.tsx now always returns commissions open. Until now they opened after 3 collected results, or once a commission had been used.
- **The profile stays in the menu before it opens.** It shows a lock icon and the hint "收下第一趟結果後開放". Opening it shows a locked panel that says what the page will hold, with a button to the star map. Someone else's shared profile link still opens.
- **What still opens step by step:**

  | Feature | Opens when |
  |---|---|
  | The profile | The first result is collected |
  | Supplies | 3 results are collected, or a supply has been used |
  | Deep dives | A team can reach cluster ③ |

  Supplies and deep dives are options inside the departure, not menu pages.
- An existing player who had not opened commissions yet sees the "每日委託" notice once.

### Summon page, "全服卡片數量"
- **The bug:** the panel shared the class `.v3-supply` with the supply dialog. v3.css limits that class to 560px wide, so the panel was squeezed into the left side, and its labels wrapped one character per line.
- **The fix:**
  - The panel's classes are now `.v3-cardcap*`, so the panel takes the full row with its three kinds side by side.
  - Labels no longer wrap.
  - The numbers inside each kind fill the row.

### Generic decision cards redrawn (event-00 to 03)
- The founder redrew the four cards that can appear in any cluster: 光橋裂紋, 迷途訊號, 星塵亂流 and 共鳴雜音. They are now set in neutral deep space with a white light bridge, with no cluster's colours or landmarks.
- The 10-21 originals moved to `docs/lightdive/art/incoming/_replaced-2026-10-22/`. LOG.md has 4 new rows.

Translations: i18n `v43.ts` (4 rows).

Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
