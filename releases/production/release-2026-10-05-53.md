## The holes

Both are "pick your result" holes, and both come from the neutral fallback the contracts apply when nobody reveals for a day.

1. **Dives.** `Expedition3.revealFallback` settles an unrevealed dive at ×1.00 after a day.
   - A result can be computed as soon as its drand round is out; the game already shows it as "待收取 +x".
   - Only the owner ever revealed. An owner could therefore leave an empty or partial result unrevealed and take ×1.00 instead.
2. **Summons.** `LightdiveMinter3.revealFallback` draws with the request's own entropy after a day. That entropy is computable at request time.
   - A summoner could compare the drand draw with the fallback draw and keep the better one.
   - The day is a constant in the contract.

## What changed

### Automatic reveals (the keeper)
- **Code and schedule.** The keeper is `lib/lightdive-v3/keeper.ts`, run by the Worker's cron. The schedule `*/2 * * * *` is in `wrangler.production.jsonc`, and `scheduled` in `scripts/production-worker.mjs` calls it.
- **What it does on each run:**
  - Reveals every dive and summon whose drand round is out, using the round's signature.
  - Simulates each one first and skips anything its owner just revealed.
  - Reveals dives in batches of 20; summons one per transaction, up to 10 per run.
  - Keeps a cursor and a status in the `settings` table.
- **Rewards and cards** still go to their owners. The keeper wallet only pays gas.
- **Off until set up.** The keeper does nothing without the Worker secret `LIGHTDIVE_KEEPER_KEY`, a wallet the founder creates and funds. Nobody else handles the key.
- **Status.** `GET /api/lightdive/keeper` returns the status. /admin/lightdive shows its last run, counts, wallet and balance, or a red warning while it is off.
- **Test.** `scripts/lightdive-v3-keeper-test.mjs` runs on a local chain with real drand. It passed:
  - 3 summons were revealed by the keeper.
  - A dive was revealed by the keeper, equal to the website's prediction, with the reward credited to its owner.
  - A dive its owner revealed first was skipped without an error.

### Owner settings page
- `scripts/lightdive-v3-owner-web.mjs` (`npm run lightdive:v3:owner:web`, http://127.0.0.1:8791) prepares settings transactions for the founder to sign in MetaMask, then checks the sender, target, data and receipt.
- It prepares one change: `setTiming` with the fallback moving from 24 hours to 7 days, the contract's maximum. `--passes N` optionally adds `setLimits`.
- A read-only check against the testnet showed the current value is 24 hours, and the transaction estimates successfully from the owner.

Translations: i18n `v44.ts` (4 rows).

Vars were kept (`--keep-vars`).
