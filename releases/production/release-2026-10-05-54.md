### What changed in the contracts

- **Lazy settlement.** A dive's result and a summon's cards are fixed by their drand round, and drand signatures are public. Every signed action carries `Settle {dives, diveProofs, summons, summonProofs}` and settles those first:
  - `dive`, `diveMany`, `setTeam` and `claim` on `Expedition3`
  - `summon` on the minter
  - `list` and `listMany` on the market
  - Anyone may also call `settle`.
- **Sealed cards.** `summon` mints its cards at once with rarity 255 (`mintSealed`). They are unsealed from the drand round (`unseal`).
  - A sealed card cannot join a team or be listed.
- **Independent draws.** The shared decks are gone. Each card depends only on its own round, request and index.
  - `rarityOdds(kind)` replaces `deckOf`.
  - Odds are unchanged: 48/29/15/7/1, 50/28/15/6/1 and 44/35/15/5/1 for spires, prisms and seekers.
- **Fallbacks are never better than a draw, and only the owner may use them:**
  - A dive with no proof after `fallbackSeconds` settles empty-handed with 3 wear (1 with lamp oil).
  - A summon with no proof after 7 days unseals as the weakest cards.
  - Owner-only, so nobody can erase a result the owner has seen but not yet settled.
- **Decisions and the deep-dive turn-back are removed.** `decide`, `turnBack` and `revealMany` are gone.
- **Commissions:**
  - Taken with the dive (`commission` parameter, 255 for none) via `acceptFor`.
  - Completed at settlement via `settled`, so there is no `complete` transaction.
  - The decision kind became BOND: a crew bond of at least 1–3.
- **NFT metadata.** No contract change: `setBaseURI` was one of the signed steps.

### Results counted at once
- **`use-chain.ts`:**
  - Predicts every due dive and summon from drand and counts it: rewards, wear and docking as `_wear` applies them, the crew bond, sealed cards shown as drawn, and commission completion with its bonus.
  - Builds the `Settle` for the player's next transaction.
  - A commission is chosen locally and sent with the next dive.
- **Display:**
  - The treasury tile "待寫入鏈上" shows what the next transaction will write.
  - The reveal says it is already in the treasury.
- **`onchain.ts`:**
  - `drawCard`, `cardSeed` and `predictSummon` mirror the minter.
  - `settleOf` builds the reveals a transaction carries.
  - drand signatures are cached.
  - `decideFeed` shows anyone's decided results in the live log and on the wall.
  - Decks, decisions and turn-back were dropped from the reads.
- **Other files:** `stats.ts` and `history.ts` were updated (outcomes, unsealed rarities).
- **Preview:** the preview (`sim.ts`) follows the same rules. Old saves with uncollected results are credited on load.

### The voyage as a story
- **Length:** about 26 log entries per 5-minute voyage, up from about 15.
- **New entries:**
  - two three-line short stories: 5 per cluster and 6 general
  - the event card told as a story, with a crew member saying one of its lines (words only)
  - one of 12 aftermath lines
  - Rovyn from the observatory (14 lines)
- **Source and output:** the text is in `docs/lightdive/TEXTS-v3-draft.md` section 八, and `scripts/lightdive-v3-texts.mjs` generates it.
- **Guides:** the whitepaper (3 languages), the guide, the coach and the unlock texts describe the new flow.

### New features
- **G3 觀測牆:** `/game/lightdive/wall`, a big screen with no wallet.
  - It shows today's numbers, bridges in flight with countdowns, the latest reveal in large, recent reveals, the sky and summons.
  - It refreshes every 10 s, and the live log links to it.
- **G5 世界觀投票:** the menu view `vote`.
  - One question a month (`lib/lightdive-v3/votes.ts`: 2026-10, 2026-11 and 2026-12 are drafts).
  - One vote per wallet, changeable until the month ends.
  - Counts show after voting, and last month's result is shown.
  - It changes no rule, odd or reward.
- **Vote API:**
  - `GET /api/lightdive/vote?month=` returns the counts (30 s edge cache) and, when signed in, the wallet's own choice.
  - `POST /api/lightdive/vote` needs the wallet session and the current month; the same-origin and rate limits apply.
- **卡片資料:** `GET /api/lightdive/nft/{id}` returns ERC-721 metadata from the chain: name, description, card art, rarity, power, berths, beam or light, voyages left and status.
  - Sealed cards show the card back.
  - It is cached for 300 s, or 30 s while sealed.

Translations: i18n `v45.ts` (244 rows). `v44.ts` (keeper) was removed. zh-Hans is generated (`壺→壶` added to the table).
